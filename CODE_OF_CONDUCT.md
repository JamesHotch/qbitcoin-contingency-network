# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We aim for a technically rigorous, patient, and inclusive research community.
Participants should:

- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- State uncertainty and conflicts of interest.
- Welcome questions and explain specialized terminology.
- Respect privacy and coordinated vulnerability disclosure.
- Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, and
  investment solicitation.
- Never pressure others to run software with real value.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
for conduct that undermines a safe research environment. Serious security
matters should use the private process in `SECURITY.md`; other conduct reports
may be made through a GitHub issue that omits private or sensitive information.

Maintainers will seek context, protect reporter privacy where practical, avoid
retaliation, and apply proportionate responses. This policy applies in project
spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
